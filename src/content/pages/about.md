I'm **Husni Rizal** — a web3 developer based on (and often returning to) an
island in Indonesia. I work mostly in Substrate, EVM, and the wider Polkadot
ecosystem. Off-keyboard, I'm chasing stoicism, learning math and physics, and
keeping a small slice of life offline.

This site is where I write down what I'm learning. The aim is honesty over
polish — I'd rather publish a partial understanding I can come back to than
sit on a "proper" draft forever.

If something here was useful, weird, or wrong, the comment box at the bottom
of every post is the best way to tell me.

— Husni
